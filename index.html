Bloc 1 — Priorité maximale

Les Temps (Tenses)

Présent dans quasiment tous les sujets depuis 10 ans. Le piège For/Since/Ago est le n°1 absolu.

~25% du QCM

1.1 Present Simple vs Present Continuous

▼
La question du concours n'est jamais "conjugue ce verbe". C'est toujours "choisis LE BON TEMPS dans ce contexte".

Present Simple — Ce qui est permanent

Sujet + V (+ -s à he/she/it)
UsageExempleSignal
HabitudeI wake up at 7 every day.every, always, usually, often
Vérité généraleWater boils at 100°C.faits scientifiques
Horaire fixeThe train leaves at 9:15.horaires, programmes
Stative verbsI know the answer.verbes d'état (voir liste)

Present Continuous — Ce qui se passe maintenant ou est temporaire

Sujet + am/is/are + V-ing
UsageExempleSignal
Action en coursShe is working right now.now, right now, at the moment
TemporaireI'm staying at a hotel this week.this week, these days, currently
Plan futur arrangéWe're meeting them tomorrow.date/lieu/heure précis
TendancePrices are rising.changement en cours
⚠️ PIÈGE N°1 — LES STATIVE VERBS

Ces verbes REFUSENT le continuous. Jamais de -ing :

Pensée : know, believe, understand, remember, think (= croire), suppose, realize

Émotions : love, hate, like, want, need, wish, prefer

Sens : see, hear, smell, taste (perception involontaire)

Possession : have (= posséder), own, belong

Autres : be, seem, mean, cost, contain

I am knowing the answer. → I know the answer.

⚠️ PIÈGE N°2 — DOUBLE SENS

Certains verbes changent de sens selon la forme :

I think you're right. → opinion (stative) → Simple

I'm thinking about changing jobs. → réflexion active → Continuous

I have a car. → possession → Simple

I'm having lunch. → action → Continuous

💡 TECHNIQUE EXPRESS

Traduis le verbe avec "être en train de" en français. Si ça sonne bizarre → Present Simple.

"Je suis en train de savoir" ? Bizarre → I know ✅

"Je suis en train de travailler" ? Normal → I'm working ✅

⚠️ PIÈGE N°3 — LE -S À LA 3e PERSONNE

Au concours, élimine systématiquement les propositions qui oublient le -s :

He think → He thinks

She want → She wants

C'est du point gratuit : si une option n'a pas le -s, elle est fausse.

1.2 Past Simple vs Past Continuous

▼

Past Simple — Action terminée, moment précis

V-ed (réguliers) ou 2e colonne (irréguliers)

Signaux : yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago, then, suddenly

I graduated in 2023.

She called me at 3pm.

He woke up, got dressed and left. (succession)

Past Continuous — Le décor, l'action en cours dans le passé

was/were + V-ing

Signaux : while, when (+ interruption), as

I was studying when the phone rang.

While she was cooking, he was cleaning.

⚠️ PIÈGE N°4 — WHEN + QUEL TEMPS ?

When I arrived, she was cooking. → J'arrive pendant qu'elle cuisinait

When I arrived, she cooked dinner. → J'arrive, puis elle a cuisiné (succession)

Règle : "While" = TOUJOURS Past Continuous. "When" = dépend du contexte.

1.3 Present Perfect vs Past Simple — LE PIÈGE N°1 DU CONCOURS

▼
🚨 C'est LE piège récurrent, présent dans quasiment tous les sujets depuis 10 ans. Maîtrise cette partie et tu sécurises 5 à 8 points sur 40.

Present Perfect — Le passé connecté au présent

have/has + past participle (3e colonne)
UsageSignauxExemple
Expérience de vieever, neverHave you ever been to London?
Résultat présentjust, already, yetI've just finished.
Durée jusqu'à maintenantsince, forShe has lived here since 2019.
Superlatif + ever—It's the best film I've ever seen.
⚠️ PIÈGE N°5 — FOR vs SINCE vs AGO

FOR + durée : for 3 days, for 2 years, for a long time

SINCE + moment précis : since Monday, since 2020, since I was born

AGO + durée (TOUJOURS Past Simple) : I moved here 3 years ago.

I am living in Paris since 10 years.

I have been living in Paris for 10 years.

💡 RÉFLEXE QCM

Tu vois since / for / ever / never / just / already / yet → cherche le Present Perfect dans les options. Taux de réussite : ~90%.

Tu vois ago / yesterday / last / in 2020 → cherche le Past Simple.

La différence cruciale

Present PerfectPast Simple
I've been to London.

moment précis
She's worked here for 3 years.
elle y travaille encore		She worked here for 3 years.
elle n'y travaille plus

1.4 Present Perfect Continuous & Past Perfect

▼

Present Perfect Continuous — Durée/processus

have/has + been + V-ing
Perfect Simple (résultat)Perfect Continuous (durée)
I've read 3 books. → résultat chiffréI've been reading all day. → insiste sur le temps passé
She's written the report. → c'est finiShe's been writing. → peut-être encore dessus
💡 RÈGLE RAPIDE

Résultat / quantité → Perfect Simple

Durée / processus → Perfect Continuous

Past Perfect — Le passé du passé

had + past participle

Sert à montrer qu'une action s'est passée AVANT une autre action passée.

When I arrived, she had already left.

I realized I had forgotten my keys.

⚠️ PIÈGE N°6 — HARDLY/SCARCELY/NO SOONER

Structure d'inversion fréquente au concours SESAME :

Hardly had he seen the dog, when he ran away.

No sooner had I arrived than it started raining.

Hardly/Scarcely → when

No sooner → than

⚠️ Ne confonds jamais : Hardly... than / No sooner... when

1.5 Le Futur — Will vs Going to vs Present Continuous

▼
FormeUsageExemple
will + VDécision spontanée, prédiction, promesseI'll help you.
be going to + VIntention planifiée, prédiction sur preuvesLook! It's going to rain.
Present ContinuousPlan arrangé avec détailsI'm meeting John at 5pm.
⚠️ PIÈGE N°7 — WILL vs GOING TO

Preuves visibles → going to : She's going to have a baby.

Décision à l'instant → will : "Phone's ringing." — "I'll answer it."

Promesse → will : I will never forget you.

🧪 Quiz Bloc 1 — Teste-toi (type concours)

▼

Questions type SESAME — Tenses

1. "It's high time you _______ listening to the good advice you are being given."

A) start B) started C) would start D) have started
✅ "It's high time" + PAST → c'est une structure fixe. Même si le sens est présent/futur, on met le prétérit. C'est un piège classique SESAME. Aussi : "advices" n'existe pas → "advice" est indénombrable.

2. "Hardly had he seen the dog, _______ away."

A) he runs B) when he ran C) than he ran D) when he runs
✅ "Hardly" → toujours suivi de "when" (pas "than"). Et le verbe doit être au prétérit (ran) pour correspondre au past perfect "had seen". Question tombée au concours SESAME officiel.

3. "She _______ in Paris for 10 years."

A) is living B) lives C) has been living D) lived
✅ "for 10 years" + action qui continue → Present Perfect Continuous. C'est LE piège n°1 du concours. "is living for..." est la faute française classique.

4. "If I _______ you, I would accept the offer."

A) were B) was C) am D) would be
✅ Conditionnel type 2 → "If I were" (pas "was" au concours). Le subjonctif "were" est obligatoire pour tous les sujets dans la structure "If I were you".

5. "By the time we arrived, the film _______."

A) started B) has started C) was starting D) had already started
✅ "By the time we arrived" (passé) → l'action du film est AVANT → Past Perfect. Le film avait commencé avant notre arrivée.

6. "She _______ three languages fluently."

A) is speaking B) speaks C) is speak D) speaking
✅ Capacité permanente → Present Simple. "Speak" dans le sens de "maîtriser une langue" est une capacité, pas une action en cours.

7. "I _______ to Japan. I'd love to go someday."

A) didn't go B) wasn't going C) have never been D) never went
✅ Expérience de vie sans date précise + "never" → Present Perfect. "I'd love to go" confirme le lien avec le présent.

8. "No sooner had she left _______ it started to rain."

A) than B) when C) that D) before
✅ "No sooner... than" — structure fixe. Ne confonds pas avec "Hardly... when". Piège récurrent au SESAME.

Bloc 4 — Points sûrs

Modaux, Conditionnels, Passif & Reported Speech

Les modaux et conditionnels sont des "règles finies" : une fois apprises, c'est du point garanti.

~15% du QCM

4.1 Modal Verbs — Tableau de synthèse

▼
ModalSensExemple
cancapacité, permissionI can swim.
couldcapacité passée, politesse, possibilitéCould you help me?
maypermission (formel), possibilitéIt may rain.
mightpossibilité (moins sûr)He might come.
mustobligation forte, déductionYou must wear a seatbelt.
shouldconseilYou should study more.
shallproposition (brit.)Shall we go?
⚠️ PIÈGE N°12 — MUST vs HAVE TO vs MUSTN'T vs DON'T HAVE TO

must = obligation personnelle : I must stop smoking.

have to = obligation externe : I have to wear a uniform.

mustn't = INTERDICTION : You mustn't cheat.

don't have to = PAS OBLIGÉ (≠ mustn't) : You don't have to come.

⚠️ La différence mustn't ≠ don't have to est un piège classique. L'un interdit, l'autre donne le choix.

⚠️ PIÈGE N°13 — MUST (déduction)

must + V = déduction présente : He must be at home. (= il est sûrement...)

must have + PP = déduction passée : She must have forgotten.

can't have + PP = impossibilité passée : He can't have said that.

4.2 Conditionals — Les 4 Types

▼
TypeIf-clauseMain clauseUsage
0If + presentpresentVérité générale
1If + presentwill + VProbable
2If + pastwould + VIrréel présent
3If + past perfectwould have + PPIrréel passé

Type 0 : If you heat water, it boils.

Type 1 : If it rains, I will stay home.

Type 2 : If I had money, I would travel.

Type 3 : If I had studied, I would have passed.

⚠️ PIÈGE N°14 — JAMAIS "WILL" APRÈS "IF"

If it will rain → If it rains

Exception unique : si "if" = "whether" (= savoir si) : I don't know if he will come.

⚠️ PIÈGE N°15 — IF I WERE (pas WAS)

Au conditionnel 2, were pour TOUS les sujets :

If I were you, I would accept.

If she were here, she would know.

Au concours, "were" est TOUJOURS la réponse attendue.

Structures utiles

Unless = if... not : Unless you hurry, you'll be late.

Provided that / As long as : You can go, provided that you come back by 10.

5.2 Gerund vs Infinitive — La règle définitive

▼

Verbes + GERUND (V-ing)

Mnémotechnique : MEGAFAPS → Mind, Enjoy, Give up, Avoid, Finish, Admit, Postpone, Suggest

+ enjoy, consider, deny, risk, keep (on), imagine, can't help, be worth, be used to, look forward to

Verbes + INFINITIVE (to + V)

want, need, decide, hope, expect, plan, agree, refuse, offer, promise, manage, afford, learn, pretend, seem

Verbes avec changement de sens

Verbe+ Gerund+ Infinitive
stopStop smoking. = arrêter de fumerStop to smoke. = s'arrêter pour fumer
rememberI remember locking. = souvenir passéRemember to lock. = n'oublie pas
tryTry eating less. = testeTry to eat less. = fais un effort
regretI regret telling her. = je regretteI regret to inform you. = formel
⚠️ PIÈGE N°17 — PRÉPOSITION + TOUJOURS GERUND

Après une préposition, c'est TOUJOURS le gerund :

She is good at swimming.

I'm interested in learning.

Thank you for helping.

I look forward to meeting you. (to = préposition ici, pas infinitif !)

Thank you for to help → FAUX

I look forward to meet you → FAUX

🧪 Quiz Final — 10 Questions type SESAME (Blocs 1-5)

▼

Simule les conditions : 45 secondes par question

1. "She is very _______ (create)."

A) creation B) creatively C) creative D) create
✅ Après "is very" → adjectif. Creative = adjectif. Creation = nom. Creatively = adverbe.

2. "_______ the bad weather, the event was a success."

A) Although B) Despite C) However D) Nevertheless
✅ "the bad weather" = nom → Despite + nom. Although exigerait une phrase complète ("Although the weather was bad").

3. "You _______ come if you don't want to."

A) mustn't B) can't C) shouldn't D) don't have to
✅ "if you don't want to" → pas d'obligation = don't have to. Mustn't = interdiction (sens opposé).

4. "I look forward to _______ you at the conference."

A) meeting B) meet C) have met D) met
✅ "look forward to" → "to" est une PRÉPOSITION ici, pas un infinitif. Après une préposition → toujours V-ing.

5. "The teacher asked me where I _______."

A) did live B) lived C) do live D) was living
✅ Reported question → ordre affirmatif (pas d'inversion, pas de do/did). "Where do you live?" → asked where I lived.

6. "He _______ pretend to be sick; he actually was."

A) didn't B) doesn't C) wasn't D) isn't
✅ "pretend" = faire semblant (faux ami). "wasn't pretending" = il ne faisait pas semblant → il était vraiment malade. Test de faux ami + temps.

7. "If I had known earlier, I _______ differently."

A) would have acted B) would act C) will act D) acted
✅ "If I had known" = conditionnel type 3 (irréel passé) → would have + past participle.

8. "Can I have some _______, please?"

A) advices B) an advice C) advise D) advice
✅ "advice" est indénombrable → pas de "s", pas de "an". "Advise" = le verbe (conseiller), "advice" = le nom (un conseil).

9. "The company was _______ in 2010."

A) set on B) set up C) set off D) set out
✅ "set up" = créer, fonder. "set off" = déclencher/partir. "set out" = se mettre en route. "set on" = peu utilisé.

10. "She _______ to the meeting, but she had a flat tyre."

A) should come B) must come C) was supposed to come D) will come
✅ "was supposed to" = elle devait/était censée venir (mais ne l'a pas fait). Le past simple "had" confirme qu'on est dans le passé.
